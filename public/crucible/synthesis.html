Task and environment synthesis

From a request to a perfect RL task

The diagram follows one task from a domain request to a certified, trainable task. Planted defects then measure the pipeline, and a regularised loop improves it. One accounts payable example runs through every step.

Property 1

Valid

None of eleven defect classes is present. A certificate bounds the rate.

Property 2

Learnable

The student succeeds between 10 and 60 percent of the time.

Property 3

On intent

Success needs the target skill. Removing its evidence breaks success.

Property 4

Novel

The task adds a behaviour that the pool lacks.

    The eleven defects a task can carry
